# Contoso Leave Policy (2026)

**Audience:** All full-time Contoso employees  
**Owner:** People Operations  
**Last updated:** 12 January 2026

## Annual leave entitlement

| Tenure | Days per calendar year |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 years | 20 days |
| 3–5 years | 22 days |
| 6+ years | 25 days |

Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on contracted hours.

## Booking rules

1. Requests must be submitted in **Workday** at least **5 business days** before the leave start date.
2. Managers should approve or decline within **2 business days**.
3. No more than **10 consecutive working days** may be taken without VP approval.
4. The team must keep at least **one coverage person** available for production support during leave.

## Carry-over

Unused leave may carry over up to **5 days** into the next calendar year. Carried days expire on **31 March**.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to **10 sick days** per year without a medical certificate. Absences longer than **3 consecutive days** require a doctor's note uploaded to Workday.

## Public holidays

Public holidays are observed according to the employee's primary work location and do **not** count against annual leave.
